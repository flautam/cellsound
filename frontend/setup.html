Tones and pulses

Each cluster has its own tone, so you can tell groups apart the way you tell tones apart on a hearing test.

Faster pulses mean closer to the Point of Audio. Farther cells tap more slowly. The tone itself stays that cluster's note.

Chunks are spatial neighborhoods (fixed-radius circles around each chunk center). More clusters means more neighborhoods across the field.
